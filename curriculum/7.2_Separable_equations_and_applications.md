## 1. What a separable equation is (slide 2)

A separable equation is a first-order differential equation in which the
expression for $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ can be factored as a function of $x$ times a
function of $y$:
$$\frac{dy}{dx}=g(x)\cdot f(y)$$
Examples on the slide: $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac yx$, $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\sin x\cdot\cos y$,
$(x^2+1)y'=2xy$, $y'=2^{x+y}$.

## 2. How to solve it (slides 3-9)

**Method (slide 3).** Divide by $f(y)$, multiply by $dx$, integrate both sides:
$$\frac{dy}{dx}=g(x)f(y)\ \Rightarrow\ \frac{1}{f(y)}\,dy=g(x)\,dx\ \Rightarrow\ \int\frac{1}{f(y)}\,dy=\int g(x)\,dx$$
Separating the slide's four examples:
$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac yx\Rightarrow\dfrac{dy}{y}=\dfrac{dx}{x}$;
$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\sin x\cos y\Rightarrow\dfrac{dy}{\cos y}=\sin x\,dx$;
$(x^2+1)y'=2xy\Rightarrow\dfrac{dy}{y}=\dfrac{2x}{x^2+1}dx$;
$y'=2^{x+y}\Rightarrow\dfrac{dy}{dx}=2^x\cdot2^y\Rightarrow\dfrac{dy}{2^y}=2^x\,dx$.

**Example 1 (slides 4-5).** (1) Solve $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{x^2}{y^2}$. (2) Find
the solution that satisfies $y(0)=2$.
(1) $y^2\,dy=x^2\,dx\Rightarrow\int y^2dy=\int x^2dx\Rightarrow\frac13y^3+C_1=\frac13x^3+C_2$.
One constant is enough on one side: $C=C_2-C_1$, so $\frac13y^3=\frac13x^3+C$,
i.e. $y=\sqrt[3]{x^3+3C}=\sqrt[3]{x^3+K}$ with $K$ an arbitrary constant.
(2) $y(0)=\sqrt[3]{K}=2\Rightarrow K=8$, so $y=\sqrt[3]{x^3+8}$.

**Example 2 (slide 6).** Solve $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{6x^2}{2y+\cos y}$.
$(2y+\cos y)\,dy=6x^2\,dx\Rightarrow\int(2y+\cos y)dy=\int6x^2dx\Rightarrow y^2+\sin y=2x^3+C$.
The general solution is given in implicit form: $y$ cannot be expressed
explicitly as a function of $x$.

**Example 3 (slide 7).** Solve $y'=x^2y$. In Leibniz notation $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=x^2y$;
if $y\ne0$: $\dfrac{dy}{y}=x^2dx\Rightarrow\ln|y|=\dfrac{x^3}{3}+C\Rightarrow|y|=e^{x^3/3+C}\Rightarrow y=\pm e^Ce^{x^3/3}\Rightarrow y=Ae^{x^3/3}$,
where $A$ is an arbitrary constant.

**Example 4 (slides 8-9), a mixing problem.** A tank contains 20 kg of salt
dissolved in 5000 L of water. Brine that contains 0.03 kg of salt per liter
enters the tank at 25 L/min. The solution is kept thoroughly mixed and drains
from the tank at the same rate. How much salt remains in the tank after half an
hour? Let $y(t)$ be the kilograms of salt after $t$ minutes: $y(0)=20$, find
$y(30)$. $\dfrac{dy}{dt}=(\text{rate in})-(\text{rate out})$, where
rate in $=0.03\frac{\text{kg}}{\text{L}}\cdot25\frac{\text{L}}{\text{min}}=0.75\frac{\text{kg}}{\text{min}}$
and rate out $=\frac{y(t)}{5000}\frac{\text{kg}}{\text{L}}\cdot25\frac{\text{L}}{\text{min}}=\frac{y(t)}{200}\frac{\text{kg}}{\text{min}}$.
So $\dfrac{dy}{dt}=\dfrac{150-y(t)}{200}$. Separating:
$\int\dfrac{dy}{150-y}=\int\dfrac{dt}{200}\Rightarrow-\ln|150-y|=\dfrac t{200}+C$.
From $y(0)=20$: $-\ln130=C$, so $|150-y|=130e^{-t/200}$; since $150-y>0$,
$$y(t)=150-130e^{-t/200},\qquad y(30)=150-130e^{-30/200}\approx38.1\text{ kg}$$

**Exercises 1 (slide 10).** Solve the given differential equations:
(1) $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=xy$; (2) $\dfrac{dv}{ds}=\dfrac{s+1}{sv+s}$;
(3) $y'\tan x=a+y,\ y\!\left(\frac\pi3\right)=a$; (4) $\dfrac{dP}{dt}=\sqrt{Pt},\ P(1)=2$;

**Exercises 2 (slide 11).** (1) Find the function such that
$f'(x)=f(x)(1-f(x))$ and $f(0)=\frac12$. (2) Solve $y'=x+y$ by making the
change of variable $u=x+y$. (3) Find an equation of the curve that passes
through the point $(0,1)$ and whose slope at $(x,y)$ is $xy$.

**Exercises 3 (slide 12).** An integral equation is an equation that contains
an unknown function $y(x)$ and an integral that involves $y(x)$. Solve the given
integral equation. [Hint: use an initial condition obtained from the integral
equation.] (1) $y(x)=2+\displaystyle\int_2^x[t-ty(t)]\,dt$;
(2) $y(x)=2+\displaystyle\int_1^x\frac{dt}{ty(t)},\ x>0$.
