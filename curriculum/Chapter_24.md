**1.** The charge on the square plates of a parallel-plate capacitor is Q.
The potential across the plates is maintained with constant voltage by a
battery as they are pulled apart to twice their original separation, which is
small compared to the dimensions of the plates. The amount of charge on the
plates is now equal to
A) 4Q  B) 2Q  C) Q  D) Q/2

**2.** The electric field between the square plates of a parallel-plate
capacitor has magnitude E. The potential across the plates is maintained with
constant voltage by a battery as they are pulled apart to twice their original
separation, which is small compared to the dimensions of the plates. The
magnitude of the electric field between the plates is now equal to
A) 4E  B) 2E  C) E  D) E/2

**3.** Equal but opposite charges Q are placed on the square plates of an
air-filled parallel-plate capacitor. The plates are then pulled apart to twice

A) The energy density in the capacitor has increased.
B) The electric field between the plates has increased.
C) The potential difference across the plates has doubled.
D) The capacitance has doubled.
