## 1. Greatest Common Divisor (GCD)

Calculate the GCD of two positive integers *a* and *b* (1 ≤ a, b ≤ 1000).

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| 25 75 | GCD 25 |
| 2534 1429 | GCD 1 |
| 1562 33 | GCD 11 |

## 2. Perfect Number Checker

A perfect number equals the sum of its proper positive divisors (excluding itself). E.g. 6 is perfect: 1+2+3=6.

Read a positive integer n (n > 1); print "Perfect number" or "Not a perfect number".

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| 28 | Perfect number |
| 12 | Not a perfect number |
| 496 | Perfect number |
